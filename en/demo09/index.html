01｜The practical question

Vision and speech models require correctly decoded pixels and audio samples. Before neural inference, a pipeline should verify dimensions, color statistics, waveform format and spectral measurements instead of trusting file names.

02｜How the system works

Pillow writes and reopens RGB PNGs, NumPy generates a sine-wave signal, the standard wave module stores PCM16 WAV, and NumPy FFT measures the recovered audio spectrum and RMS. No pretrained image or speech model runs.

03｜Experiment inputs and execution

A 120×80 PNG includes a red rectangle of 2,400 pixels; a mono 16 kHz one-second WAV contains a 440 Hz sine wave. Both are saved and reopened, with red-pixel area, dominant frequency, duration and RMS checked.

04｜What acceptance actually observed

Real file and signal-processing checks passed, producing PNG/WAV fixtures and IMAGE_AUDIO_SIGNAL_E2E_PASS evidence. Synthetic fixtures and absence of a pretrained vision or speech model are explicitly recorded.

06｜Limits and future deepening

This is not OCR, semantic vision, ASR, speaker identification or an evaluation under noisy real recordings. It is signal-processing infrastructure for later multimodal pipelines.
