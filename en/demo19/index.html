01｜The problem to solve

First-stage retrieval produces candidate passages, often without ideal ordering. Cross-Encoders jointly read a query and one candidate at a time to score pairwise relevance and reorder the shortlist.

02｜How the technology works

The genuine pretrained cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L6-v2 model runs a joint query–passage Transformer classification head with a pinned source revision. It produces raw relevance logits rather than independent embeddings, BM25 ranks or calibrated probabilities.

03｜Actual experiment inputs

Two constructed English queries share the same five initial passages. Expected document identifiers are excluded from model inputs and ranking. Exact query–passage pairs, tensor shapes, raw logits and final ranking evidence are saved.

04｜Observed result and independent evidence

The trace-inspection query ranked DOC-JAEGER first (raw logit 3.5580726); scanned-PDF OCR ranked DOC-OCR first (raw logit 9.7813416). Both rankings and independent recomputation passed.

Source and Actions may require private-repository access; this public lesson contains no redistributed model weights or private data.

06｜What cannot be concluded

Five supplied candidates omit first-stage retrieval and do not form a complete RAG system. Raw logits are not calibrated probabilities; two constructed queries do not establish MRR/NDCG or production ranking quality.
