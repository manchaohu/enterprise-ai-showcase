01｜The problem to solve

ASR asks what was spoken; multi-speaker systems additionally need to distinguish who spoke. A bounded experiment can start with known segment boundaries and compare pretrained speaker embeddings, without attempting blind meeting segmentation.

02｜How the technology works

The genuine SpeechBrain spkrec-xvect-voxceleb pretrained model runs CPU inference on presegmented speech to derive X-vectors. Cosine similarity supports known-speaker attribution, and a separate verifier checks saved numeric evidence.

03｜Actual experiment inputs

Three recordings with known speech boundaries are embedded and compared. Earlier documentation described model-availability preflight only; the later successful dedicated E2E executed actual pretrained embedding inference, a distinct stage.

04｜Observed result and independent evidence

The dedicated Actions run verified genuine pretrained X-vector computation and numerical output checks. The result supports known-segment attribution, not unconstrained diarization of arbitrary multi-speaker recordings.

06｜What cannot be concluded

Three known-boundary samples do not evaluate blind turn segmentation, overlapping speakers, multilingual generalization, diarization error rate (DER) or production speaker accuracy and media redistribution rights.
