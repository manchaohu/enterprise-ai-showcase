01｜The practical question

Two sentences can mean nearly the same thing without sharing exact wording. Text embedding models map sentences to vectors so similarity comparisons can reflect learned semantic patterns.

02｜How the system works

The genuine pretrained sentence-transformers/paraphrase-MiniLM-L3-v2 network runs on CPU and encodes sentences into normalized numerical embeddings. Cosine similarity compares two dog/puppy variants and two database/SQLite transaction variants.

03｜Experiment inputs and execution

Four synthetic English sentences are encoded. The verifier checks vector shape, finite values, normalized norms, semantically related pairs exceeding cross-topic similarity and repeat encoding stability for the first sentence.

04｜What acceptance actually observed

The verified run recorded PRETRAINED_EMBEDDING_CPU_E2E_PASS, actual model identity, pretrained weight loading, vector dimensions and similarity comparisons—distinct from the non-neural TF-IDF/SVD vectors in Demo 04.

06｜Limits and future deepening

Four English examples do not establish production retrieval recall, Chinese quality, broad semantic capability or latency targets. The requested model revision remains a moving main rather than an immutable commit.
