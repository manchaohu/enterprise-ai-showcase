How to read and check each experiment

Read the problem and mechanism, look at both explanatory illustrations and actual measured values, then inspect limitations. To reproduce a result, follow the linked source, requirements and recorded GitHub Actions checks. Diagrams are explicitly distinguished from real inference output.

Evidence policy: A successful bounded demonstration is not a measured general accuracy score, benchmark, licensing clearance or production readiness certificate. Some illustrative data is synthetic even when the model inference is genuine. The engineering repository is private, so raw source links may require permission.
